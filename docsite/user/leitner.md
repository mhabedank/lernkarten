# The Leitner box

A deck you review front to back reviews what you already know as often as what
you keep forgetting. A Leitner box fixes that with no scheduling and no app: a
card you get right moves one compartment forward and comes back less often, a
card you get wrong goes back to the first and comes back tomorrow.

`lernkarten build --grid a8 --dividers 4` prints the compartment dividers
alongside the cards, sized for the printable box.

[The method, in full](../../docs/leitner.html) — what each compartment means,
how often to work through them, and where the intervals come from.
